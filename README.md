## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1. Detailed explanation of the proposed solution
2. vadivamaippaalar
 is a user-friendly mobile application designed to connect farmers directly with customers, reducing the involvement of unnecessary intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain.

The application provides a simple digital marketplace where farmers can register, upload their agricultural products, add product images, mention prices and available quantities, and receive orders directly from customers.

Customers can browse available agricultural products, discover nearby farmers, compare prices and purchase fresh products directly through the application.

The application is designed with a simple and intuitive interface so that farmers with limited technical knowledge can easily use it.

Main Features:
- Easy farmer and customer registration.
- Farmer product upload with images, prices and quantities.
- Customer-friendly product browsing and searching.
- Nearby farmer discovery using location.
- Direct communication between farmers and customers.
- Online order placement and management.
- Delivery coordination and order tracking.
- Regional language support for rural accessibility.
2. How it addresses the problem
1. Directly connects farmers with customers without unnecessary intermediaries.
2. Helps farmers receive better prices for their products.
3. Enables customers to purchase agricultural products at transparent prices.
4. Reduces additional costs associated with multiple intermediaries.
5. Provides direct communication between farmers and customers.
6. Simplifies agricultural product purchasing through a mobile application.
3. Innovation and uniqueness of the solution
- User-Friendly Design: Simple application interface suitable for farmers and customers.
- Direct Marketplace: Farmers can sell products directly to customers.
- Nearby Farmer Discovery: Customers can find farmers in their surrounding areas.
- Transparent Pricing: Customers can view prices directly set by farmers.
- Multilingual Support: Supports regional languages for better accessibility.
- AI-Based Demand Forecasting: Helps farmers understand future product demand.
- Smart Logistics: Supports efficient delivery coordination.
